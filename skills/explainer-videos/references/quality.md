# Review the exported artifact

For each dimension record pass, needs repair, or not checked.

| Dimension | Evidence of a good result |
| --- | --- |
| Accuracy | Claims match source artifacts; proposals and illustrative examples are identified. |
| Explanation | A viewer can state the takeaway and mechanism without reading the brief. |
| Visual reasoning | Pictures carry relationships or change beyond the narration. |
| Continuity | Stable names, units, colors, and object identity across beats. |
| Readability | Labels and captions work at playback size; no clipped essentials. |
| Timing | Visuals appear with their explanation and remain long enough to inspect. |
| Audio | Narration is intelligible and complete, with no abrupt cuts or masking music. |
| Delivery | The exported video plays; dimensions, duration, and required audio were checked. |

Sample frames in every beat and on both sides of transitions, then watch the full video. Listen independently for missing words, pronunciation errors, and unsupported assertions. Compare final audio/captions rather than trusting the source script.

`inspect_video.py` checks only container/stream metadata, dimensions, duration, and presence of audio. It cannot check black frames, loudness, intelligibility, layout, or truth. State those limits honestly.
